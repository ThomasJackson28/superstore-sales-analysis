# Excel Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of 9,995 US superstore transactions to uncover 
sales performance, profit margins, and the impact of discounting.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Pivot tables with multiple value columns
- Data visualisation with charts
- Cross-referencing findings across multiple pivots
- Business insight and commentary

## Key Findings
- Technology generates the highest sales and profit
- Furniture has poor margins despite strong sales
- Discounts above 20% consistently result in losses
- West region leads in sales, Central has worst profit margin
- Consumer segment accounts for over 50% of total revenue
