# Superstore Sales Analysis

## Overview
An end-to-end analysis of 9,995 US superstore transactions to uncover 
trends in sales performance, profitability, and the impact of discounting 
across categories, regions, and customer segments.

## Dataset
- Source: Kaggle (Aman Sharma)
- 9,995 rows of US retail transaction data
- Columns include: category, region, segment, sales, profit, 
  discount, and quantity

## Key Findings
- Technology is the most profitable category
- Furniture generates strong sales but poor profit margins
- Discounts above 20% consistently result in losses
- 50% discounts result in an average loss of $310 per transaction
- West region leads in both sales and profit
- Central region has the worst profit margin despite decent sales
- Copiers and Phones are the most profitable sub-categories
- Tables and Bookcases are loss making sub-categories
